THE TOOLSET

What the agent can work with.

Computer & browser

A workspace of its own.

Research information, navigate approved websites and work through browser-based tasks. A cloud computer gives the agent a place to act, not just a place to answer.

Browser researchWeb applicationsScreen-based workflows
Example workflow

Review an enquiry, find the approved service information and prepare the next step.

The boundary Website access, accounts and permitted actions are agreed before deployment.

Terminal & files

From information to deliverables.

Read and organise files, run scoped scripts and prepare documents, spreadsheets or reports. Tools turn a written request into an output your team can inspect.

File handlingScoped scriptsReports & documents
Example workflow

Turn an approved export into a weekly report, with the source data alongside it.

The boundary Commands, folders and dependencies need explicit boundaries; unrestricted terminal access is not a default.

Email & messaging

A request becomes a job.

A dedicated inbox and configured messaging channel let people hand over work and receive updates. Telegram is part of the documented Twinthos setup; other channels are scoped for your deployment.

Email inboxTelegramHuman approvals
Example workflow

Receive a request, draft a reply and ask the owner to approve it before sending.

The boundary Reading, drafting and sending are different permissions. Slack, iMessage or phone integrations require separate confirmation.

Company knowledge

Your context, carried forward.

Approved procedures, responsibilities and project records give the agent context beyond one conversation. The Company Brain is the organised knowledge layer behind the work.

Approved sourcesProject contextPersistent records
Example workflow

Check the current policy and customer context before preparing a response.

The boundary Sources need owners, access controls and review dates. Stored information can still be wrong or out of date.

Connected tools

Work across your systems.

Connect the specific business applications the job requires through approved APIs, connectors or browser access. GitHub is part of the documented setup; your CRM or calendar is assessed individually.

APIs & connectorsGitHubBusiness applications
Example workflow

Read an approved record and prepare an update in the system your team already uses.

The boundary An available connector is not an enabled integration. Credentials and permissions are checked for each connection.

Scheduled work

Follow-through that repeats.

Run agreed tasks on a schedule, keep activity records and escalate exceptions. Software can run beyond office hours where that schedule has been configured.

Scheduled tasksActivity recordsException handling
Example workflow

Prepare the morning brief from approved sources and flag anything missing.

The boundary Run times, failure alerts and support hours are agreed in writing. This is not a promise of uninterrupted service.
