The costs around the model.

Model usage is one line in a wider operating budget.

Twinthos · Published 21 October 2026 · Updated 8 November 2026 · 1 min read

THE SHORT ANSWER

In brief

A useful quote explains who owns each recurring cost and each failure.

Build and integration

Someone must understand the workflow, connect the right accounts and test the result. Existing data may need organising before an agent can use it. Ask for a delivery scope that separates the first working version from additional tools or larger changes.

Run and review

An operating budget can include model usage, hosting, application subscriptions and human review. Costs may vary with task frequency, document size, failed attempts and the complexity of the tools involved. Request a usage assumption and a clear treatment of overages.

Maintain and recover

Credentials expire. Applications change. Policies are revised. Account for the person who maintains the workflow and investigates failures. A low initial price can be misleading if the owner later becomes an unpaid full-time operator.

Make the comparison testable

Ask for a short measurement period with agreed outputs and acceptance criteria. Track useful completed work, failures and the time people spend correcting it. Use those observations to decide whether to continue. We do not assume a salary replacement or a guaranteed saving from the presence of an agent.

IN PRACTICE / ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

The cheapest model can create an expensive workflow

A vendor may advertise a low cost per model request while omitting data preparation, account setup, monitoring and support. Consider a workflow that reads invoices: a low per-document processing price matters little if someone still needs to correct missing fields, investigate failed uploads and reconcile the accounting destination. Conversely, a simple, well-defined process can be valuable without a costly model. The economic unit is a verified completed job, not a token, a message or a claim that an agent was busy.

THE OPERATING DECISION

Assign responsibility to every cost line

Before signing, ask who pays for model usage, cloud hosting, telephony, messaging, application subscriptions and third-party connectors. Check what happens when one of those services changes price or availability. Agree when new development is an included adjustment versus a separately priced scope change. Include the responsible person's time for approvals and exceptions. Then run a limited evaluation with real acceptance criteria and keep the estimate distinct from any observed result.

DIRECT ANSWER

Why is the subscription not the full cost of an AI employee?

Because the operating system also needs permitted integrations, data, infrastructure, human oversight and maintenance. A credible quote describes these responsibilities separately.

THE PRACTICAL TEST

Request a quote that separates setup, tools, usage, maintenance and support. Ask who pays each line when activity doubles.
