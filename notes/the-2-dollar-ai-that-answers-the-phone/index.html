What an AI phone service really costs.

A cheap headline rate does not describe a complete telephone workflow.

Twinthos · Published 21 October 2026 · Updated 8 November 2026 · 1 min read

THE SHORT ANSWER

In brief

Price the whole call journey, including handoff, review and failure.

Start with the call journey

What should happen when someone rings? Is the system answering a simple question, collecting details or arranging a booking? Decide when a person needs to take over. A pleasant voice is only one part of a working service.

Ask what the rate includes

Voice processing, model usage, telephony, numbers and business-system connections may be priced separately. Ask how transferred calls, failed calls and long silences are billed. This note does not quote a current vendor rate: prices and included features must be checked with the actual supplier.

Test the uncomfortable calls

Try background noise, interruptions, unclear names and a request the system cannot fulfil. Verify that a handoff reaches the right person and that the business record accurately reflects the call. Agree the information the system should collect and avoid.

Confirm the integration

A phone capability is not included just because an agent has email or messaging. For Twinthos, voice and phone integrations require separate scoping and verification. Confirm provider terms, consent requirements and data handling for your intended use before enabling recording or automated calls.

IN PRACTICE / ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

A telephone interaction has more than one cost

Imagine a customer calling to rearrange an appointment. The phone system may need to recognise the request, establish the caller's identity where appropriate, check available slots, confirm a change and record it in the correct business system. A low quoted per-call or per-minute rate may cover only voice processing and leave the number, model, integration and human fallback as separate charges. Do not assume those components are included. Twinthos treats phone integration as an option requiring its own scoping and testing, not a default capability.

THE OPERATING DECISION

Test the conversation the provider did not script

Try a quiet caller, poor audio, interruptions, two similar names and an urgent request the service cannot safely handle. Verify both the spoken response and the actual state of the booking or CRM. Confirm where call records are stored, who may access recordings or transcripts, how callers are informed and when a person can take over. A system that apologises gracefully but leaves the wrong booking is not a successful operational outcome. The hand-off and post-call record are as important as the voice.

DIRECT ANSWER

Does a cheap AI phone price cover a complete business workflow?

Not necessarily. Ask about telephony, voice processing, model usage, external application connections, monitoring, recordings, transfer charges and human fallback.

THE PRACTICAL TEST

Try a noisy call, an interrupted caller and a request beyond the script. Verify the hand-off and the written record.
