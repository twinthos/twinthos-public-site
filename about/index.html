About

AI should make people
more capable.

Twinthos brings AI security and practical automation together. Useful agents need real tools, company context and clear accountability.

Why Twinthos exists

Twinthos takes on that repeating work so people can get back to building, serving and deciding. We do it with a digital employee: AI software that does an agreed job using your business tools, within written rules, with someone at Twinthos responsible for it.

The problem is not your people, and it is not your software. It is the gap between what the business needs doing and what anyone has time for.

How we take responsibility

One person responsible

Someone at Twinthos is responsible for keeping the agreed job working and dealing with problems.

A defined role

AI tools can do useful work. With Twinthos, we agree the job, limit what the software can use, and look after it for you.

A managed service

We do not hand you a tool to manage yourself. We take on the work. There is no new dashboard for your team to check.

Work beyond the plan

We set up and run the agreed job. The £999 operations review provides a written plan. AI security reviews are separately scoped.

Boundaries that need testing

The job specification defines permitted actions and escalation points. We test those boundaries and review failures; no AI system is guaranteed error-free.
